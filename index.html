Hello! I am a passionate software developer and digital creator behind Amber Design Studio. I specialize in designing and engineering cross-platform applications, immersive Android games, and bespoke Wear OS watch faces.

With a strong foundation in modern mobile development, C++, and UI architecture, I focus on building products that blend performance, battery efficiency, and meticulous visual detail. Every project is handled directly from concept to launch.
